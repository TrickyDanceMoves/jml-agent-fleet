Welcome
JML Fleet is a zero-trust identity lifecycle console for Microsoft Entra ID. It coordinates Joiner, Mover, and Leaver workflows across a fleet of least-privilege AI agents.

Let's take a minute to set up your operator account and connect your tenant. You can skip any step and configure it later in Settings.
